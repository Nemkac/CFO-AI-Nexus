2. Personal Data We Collect
The personal data we collect depends on how you interact with the Services and the information you choose to provide. The categories below describe the information we may collect in connection with those interactions.
2.1 Information you provide
•    Name, work email address, company, job title, country and attendee profile information.
•    Registration, ticket, order and payment-status information.
•    Billing address and applicable company, tax or VAT information.
•    Information submitted through checkout, agenda, newsletter, contact and sponsor proposal forms. Sponsor proposal information may include platform category, region, primary goal and estimated event budget.
•    Newsletter, cookie and sponsor-sharing choices.
•    Information you enter in your event profile, including industry or area of activity, functions, stated interests and any additional profile fields enabled for the event.
•    Information connected with meetings, chats, demonstration requests, QR lead scanning and other sponsor interactions.
•    Messages and other information you send to us.
2.2 Payment information
Payments are processed by Stripe. We may receive transaction information such as amount, currency, payment status and order or transaction identifiers. Full payment-card details are entered in Stripe’s payment environment and are not stored by Corporate Finance Hub.
2.3 Information collected through websites and event platforms
•    IP address, browser, device and operating-system information.
•    Pages viewed, referral source, clicks, scrolling and similar website-interaction information, subject to your cookie choices.
•    Event profile, participation, networking, meeting and platform interaction information.
•    Information generated when you use sessions, virtual booths and other event features.

We use personal data to organize and deliver our events, provide the Services you request and manage our relationship with attendees, customers and prospective sponsors. Depending on your interaction with us, we may use your personal data for the following purposes:
•    Process registrations, ticket purchases, billing and payments.
•    Provide conference and platform access and deliver requested Services.
•    Send confirmations, access instructions, schedule information and other service communications.
•    Provide agendas, programs and other requested materials.
•    Operate attendee profiles, matchmaking, networking, meetings, chats and other interactive features.
•    Send newsletters, event updates and marketing communications where permitted.
•    Record and respect newsletter, cookie and sponsor-sharing choices.

•    Respond to sponsor and partnership enquiries and conduct appropriate commercial follow-up.
•    Measure and improve website and event performance.
•    Protect the Services, prevent misuse and address security incidents.
•    Comply with legal, accounting, tax and regulatory requirements, and establish or defend legal claims.
4. Legal Bases for Processing
Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or another applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following bases, depending on the activity:
•    Contract or steps requested before entering a contract, including registration, ticket purchase and event access.
•    Consent for sponsor disclosures based on the separate sponsor-sharing option or a voluntary, informed active interaction described in Section 6, and where required for marketing and non-essential cookies.
•    Legitimate interests, where permitted, including operating and improving the Services, responding to business enquiries, maintaining security and defending legal rights, provided those interests are not overridden by individual rights.
•    Legal obligations, including applicable accounting, tax and record-keeping requirements.
Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
5. Newsletters and Sponsored Communications
We may send CFO AI Nexus newsletters, event updates and marketing communications to people who have subscribed or where otherwise permitted by applicable law. Our newsletter process uses email confirmation (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email. Where a form clearly states that requesting an agenda or other event materials includes subscribing to CFO AI Nexus communications, we use a subsequent email confirmation step to confirm that subscription.
A sponsor may provide content and a call to action for a sponsored newsletter or follow-up email. CFO AI Nexus sends those messages only to its marketing subscribers and does not provide the subscriber database to the sponsor for that purpose. If you respond to a sponsor call to action or otherwise contact the sponsor, the sponsor will receive the information you choose to provide.
Marketing choices do not affect confirmations, access instructions and other communications required to provide a Service you requested.
6. Event Sponsors and Qualified Leads
6.1 Separate sponsor-sharing choice
We do not automatically provide your contact details to sponsors because you register for an event or visit a sponsor’s virtual booth. Where a form presents a separate sponsor-sharing option, you may choose whether to allow us to share the information stated on that form with the participating sponsors identified on our Sponsors and Partners page (https://cfoainexus.com/sponsors-and-exhibitors). Depending on the form, this may include your name, work email address, company and job title. We do not share payment details with sponsors.
6.2 Active sponsor interactions and lead scanning
Where you voluntarily request a meeting, start a chat, request a demonstration or otherwise choose to establish contact with a sponsor, Brella may make the contact and profile information associated with that interaction available to the relevant sponsor. The same applies where you knowingly present or make available your QR code for lead scanning. At the relevant interaction, you will be informed that proceeding makes the indicated information available to that sponsor.
Depending on the information included in your event profile, this may include your name, email address, company, job title, industry or area of activity, stated interests, the time of the interaction and any additional profile fields you chose to complete and that are enabled for the event.
Simply visiting or viewing a sponsor’s virtual booth does not by itself cause your identifiable contact or profile information to be shared with that sponsor.
6.3 Sponsor responsibility
A sponsor that receives personal data for its own business follow-up or marketing acts as an independent controller and is responsible for its subsequent use under its privacy notice and applicable law. You may contact the sponsor directly to unsubscribe or exercise applicable rights.
If you opt in to share details with Workday, the information may be shared with Workday and other Workday group entities and used for Workday marketing, including email marketing, in accordance with Workday’s privacy notice: https://www.workday.com/en-us/privacy.html
You may withdraw sponsor-sharing consent for future disclosures at any time by contacting support@cfoainexus.com. This does not reverse a disclosure already lawfully made to a sponsor.
7. Virtual Event Platform and Matchmaking
We use Brella for attendee profiles, intent-based matchmaking, meetings, networking, chats, virtual booths and related event functions. Matchmaking is based on profile information participants choose to provide, such as industry, function and interests. This matchmaking uses information you provide in your profile, rather than tracking your behavior to infer your intentions.
Sponsor access to identifiable passive booth visitors through Brella’s Traffic Analytics functionality is disabled for CFO AI Nexus. Lead scanning is used for active interactions as described in Section 6. Corporate Finance Hub may access platform reports needed to administer and evaluate the event.
8. Event Recordings
Certain sessions or presentations may be recorded. Attendees are not automatically recorded merely by joining a session. One-to-one networking video meetings are not automatically recorded, and stream chat is not retained as a downloadable event record through the Brella administration panel.
If a session is configured so that an attendee’s voice, image or contribution may be recorded, an appropriate notice will be provided and any consent required by applicable law will be obtained. Recordings may be made available as event content or used for related educational, archival or promotional purposes consistent with the notice and the Event Terms.
9. Analytics and Cookies
Subject to your choices, we use Google Analytics to measure website traffic and Microsoft Clarity to understand interaction patterns, including clicks, scrolling, heatmaps and session replay. Clarity is configured to mask user-entered form information. These non-essential technologies are activated only after the relevant cookie consent. You can change or withdraw your choices through Cookie Settings.

We use providers to operate our websites, payments, communications and virtual events, including Stripe, Kit, Brella, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Firebase/Google, Zapier, Fastly and GoDaddy. We may also disclose personal data where required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a corporate transaction where permitted by law. Sponsor disclosures are described in Section 6.
11. Sponsor Proposal Enquiries
Information submitted through the sponsor proposal form is used by Corporate Finance Hub and authorized CFH/WTS team members to respond to the enquiry and conduct appropriate commercial follow-up. It is not provided to other sponsors or unrelated third parties, except for service providers needed to operate the form and communications.

Corporate Finance Hub LLC is established in the United States, and providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where a transfer safeguard is required under the GDPR, we use an applicable adequacy decision, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient participates, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another permitted mechanism. Appropriate UK transfer mechanisms are used where required. Under the current event setup, Brella event data is hosted on European infrastructure. You may contact us at support@cfoainexus.com to request information about the safeguards applicable to a relevant transfer and, where applicable, a copy of those safeguards.
